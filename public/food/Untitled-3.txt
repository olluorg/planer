Про воду: могу ли повторить именно этот блюпринт?
Буквально 1:1 — нет, и никто в чате не сможет. EasyWaterscape рекламирует настоящую FFT-симуляцию (спектр Tessendorf/JONSWAP → обратное БПФ на GPU каждый кадр). Их «100% Blueprint без C++» — маркетинг: FFT физически не считается в Blueprint-нодах, под капотом почти наверняка Niagara sim-stage или предрасчёт спектра в текстуры. Воспроизвести их код я не могу (да и не нужно — это их IP).

Но воссоздать тот же результат и поведение — да, двумя путями:

Путь A — расширенный Gerstner-океан (рекомендую). ~85% их картинки, 100% их поведения буйности, малой кровью.
Строим поверх того, что у тебя уже есть (HeartOcean + WaterZone + буйность из памяти):

8–12 волновых полос Gerstner (свелл + средние + чоп) с разными направлениями/крутизной → «tiling-free» вид без повторов (много направлений + доменный варп).
Foam по Якобиану — ровно их «foam where water folds»: из производных Gerstner считаем складку (det J < порога → маска пены). Это ~30 строк матанода, известная техника.
Пресеты Calm/Rough/Stormy как UDataAsset с выпадающим списком — как у них, «data-asset driven».
Одна общая волновая функция в C++ → её сэмплит и материал (через MPC), и буйность. То самое «samples live wave height anywhere», которое у тебя частично уже есть.
Путь B — настоящий Tessendorf FFT (их уровень, большой труд). Спектр Phillips/JONSWAP → IFFT через compute-шейдеры в render targets (displacement + normal + Jacobian-foam), сэмплится материалом и буйностью. Это реально в твоём C++-модуле Th, но это многосессионная стройка (кастомные global shaders / compute-пассы). В лоу-фай ночной игре разницу с хорошим Gerstner+чоп почти никто не увидит.

Мой совет: делаем A сейчас, к B возвращаемся, только если по картинке реально упрёмся. Технику берём из публичных работ (Tessendorf 2001, Gerstner, JONSWAP, Jacobian foam) — это законно, файлы Faucher не трогаем.

Hearthstream — Mechanics & Systems Spec (for Unreal port)
Engine-agnostic design + the concrete tuning values from the current Unity build, so the game can be rebuilt in Unreal with the same feel. Numbers below are the real values in the Unity scripts (file paths given). UE-specific mapping notes are in each section and collected at the end.

1. Vision & pillars
An atmospheric lo-fi ocean exploration + fishing game. A tiny boat on a vast, procedurally generated night sea. The crossbreed target:

DREDGE — stylized low-poly fishing + quiet dread, see-through water, biomes, monster.
Sid Meier's Pirates — travel/economy loop: sail named regions, dock, upgrade, sell catch.
Frostpunk — presentation & a survival pressure (here: warmth/tea instead of cold/coal), translucent minimal UI.
No combat. Relaxation + mystery. Atmosphere first: world before UI, beauty before rewards, mystery before explanation.
Feel rules (do not lose these):

Large empty space, tiny vessel, warm lantern against dark water.
All transitions are eased (SmoothStep/Lerp), never snapped — mood, weather, camera, lantern.
Translucent, sparse HUD that fades when idle. No RPG inventory clutter, no hypercasual reward spam.
2. Core gameplay loop
Sail the open sea (momentum boat). Lantern burns warmth; it slowly dims.
Stop & fish (cut engine) to land fish = soft currency. Moving scares fish.
Warmth drains → sip tea (limited reserve ~20 cups) to relight the lantern.
Reserve empties → seek an island; dock to refill the thermos, log a discovery, open the vessel/shop menu.
Spend fish on upgrades (bigger thermos, brighter lantern, better catch). Visit new regions/biomes.
Deep water hides a monster: it drags you under if you keep moving — stay still to survive. Death = respawn at dock, current catch reset.
The tea↔lantern pressure is what makes the empty sea matter and pulls the player toward islands.

3. Controls
Key	Action
W / S	Throttle forward / reverse
A / D	Steer
F	Cast line / land fish (when stopped)
E	Dock at a nearby island (when stopped)
R	Brew/sip tea (restore warmth, −1 cup)
C	Cycle camera modes
H	Cycle HUD visibility states
4. Systems
4.1 Boat & movement — _Project/Scripts/Boat/BoatController.cs
Momentum-based, not direct velocity. Drifts; turns slower at speed.

maxForwardSpeed 7.8, maxReverseSpeed 3.6, acceleration 2.6, deceleration 1.8.
turnSpeed: 42°/s at low speed → 28°/s at high speed; turnResponsiveness 3.4.
driftFactor 0.88 (velocity lerps toward heading; AutoDriftAssist doubles the correction).
minimumSteerSpeed 0.26 (can still pivot when nearly stopped).
NormalizedSpeed (0..1) drives fishing/docking "is the engine cut?" checks and the speed HUD.
Visual "mood motion": bob (amp 0.055, freq 0.42), pitch (0.65), roll (1.1) applied to a VisualRoot child on top of the authored model rotation (so the boat's facing is never overwritten).
Wave riding: each frame samples the shared wave field at bow/stern/port/starboard (hullHalfLength 2.4, hullHalfWidth 1.1) → sets hull height + pitch/roll from the water slope, so the boat physically rides the same waves the shader draws. This sync is the heart of the "feel" — keep it.

UE: Pawn + floating-pawn-style movement, or a custom movement component. For the water response, use the Water plugin buoyancy (Buoyancy Component samples the water body) — UE's strength here. Replace the manual bow/stern sampling with multiple pontoon points on the buoyancy component.

4.2 Shared ocean wave field — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/Water/OceanWaves.cs
One analytic wave function used by both the boat physics and the water shader so they never desync.

4 summed sine waves with deep-water dispersion. Amplitude 0.9 (eased toward TargetAmplitude by weather). GetHeight(worldPos, time) returns surface height.
ShallowWaterTracker eases Amplitude toward the weather's target each frame (waveAmplitudeEase 0.35).
UE: If using the Water plugin's Gerstner waves, expose the same wave params to both the buoyancy and a Material Parameter Collection so C++/Blueprint gameplay can query wave height. If hand-rolling, keep one shared HLSL/MPC wave function callable from both render and gameplay.

4.3 Water rendering — _HEARTSTREAM/Shaders/Water/S_HeartstreamWater_Prototype.shader
Stylized, see-through. Key features:

Depth-based color (scene depth) → turquoise shallows to deep navy.
Refraction via opaque scene color.
Shoreline/intersection foam and crest foam.
Vertex displacement from the shared wave field (matches OceanWaves).
Warm lantern pool painted at the boat position; daylight and warmth globals dim/tint it.
Island shallows: turquoise + foam ring around each nearby island.
Global shader params (set by C#):

Param	Meaning	Source
_HsWaveAmp	current wave amplitude	ShallowWaterTracker
_HsBoatPos	boat world pos (lantern pool)	ShallowWaterTracker
_HsLanternWarmth	0..1 lantern fuel × brightness	WarmthController
_HsDaylight	day/night factor	AtmosphereMoodController
_HsIslandShallows[8]	xz = center, w = radius (34 m)	ShallowWaterTracker
_HsIslandShallowCount	active island count (≤8)	ShallowWaterTracker
UE: Material Parameter Collection for these globals. UE's Single Layer Water + Water plugin gives you depth color, refraction, and proper buoyancy out of the box — this is the main reason to port. Island shallows = either a distance-field/decal approach or feed the same 8 island centers into the MPC.

4.4 Islands (streaming lattice) — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/World/IslandField.cs
Infinite-feeling, seeded, pooled.

Grid cell 170 m, view radius 2 cells (5×5 around the boat), density 0.22, seed 1207, cap 14.
Cell (0,0) kept clear (open spawn). Per-cell deterministic RNG → occupancy + position jitter + scale (0.7–1.6) + yaw.
Far cells recycle into a pool. Each island carries IslandInteraction (used by shallows + docking).
Visual = placeholder rounded mounds (swap for real low-poly island models).
UE: PCG framework or a custom streaming grid keyed by the player's cell; deterministic per-cell seed (hash of seed,x,y). Pool instances. Each island actor = a "DockableIsland" with a region/discovery id.

4.5 Shallows feed — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/Water/ShallowWaterTracker.cs
Scans nearest 8 islands every 1.5 s, sorted by distance, writes their xz+radius (34 m) into the shader globals; also drives _HsWaveAmp and _HsBoatPos. Keep the 8-slot limit (cheap, "what you see").

4.6 Docking — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/World/DockingController.cs + …/World/POI/IslandInteraction.cs
No physics — pure proximity.

dockRange 24 m, must be slow (NormalizedSpeed ≤ 0.12), press E.
Docking payoff (IslandInteraction.Interact): log a unique discovery (id from rounded x,z); first visit gives +2 fish and a flavor message; refill the thermos; open the vessel/shop menu.
UE: Overlap sphere on the boat vs island, or a gameplay-ability interact. Same payoff order.

4.7 Fishing — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/Fishing/FishingController.cs
Calm timing minigame. Cutting the engine is required — quietly teaches the "stay still" instinct the monster later exploits.

State machine: Idle → Casting → Waiting → Bite → Result.

Can cast only when NormalizedSpeed ≤ 0.07. Press F to cast.
castDuration 0.7 s → wait 2.2–6.5 s (random) for a bite.
Bite = reel window 1.5 s; press F again inside it to land. HUD shows a shrinking urgency bar.
Moving during cast/wait cancels ("the wake scared them off").
Result message holds 2.0 s, then back to Idle.
Species table (name, fish value, weight): Silverfin 3 / w5 · Moonperch 5 / w3 · Hollow Cod 6 / w2.5 · Dusk Eel 9 / w1.4 · Pale Lantern 14 / w0.6. Weighted random pick → AddFish(value) + RecordCatch(name).
UE: Same state machine as a simple FSM/Blueprint. Species table = DataTable. Later: bias the table by biome and by proximity to islands ("hotspots").

4.8 Tea ↔ lantern warmth — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/Atmosphere/WarmthController.cs
The survival pressure.

warmth 0..1, decays 0.02/s (~50 s full→dark if you never sip).
Lantern (boat point light) intensity = lanternMinFactor(0.06) + 0.94*warmth, × upgrade brightness. Also writes _HsLanternWarmth = warmth × brightness to the water shader.
R brews: −1 cup, warmth→1, sipCooldown 1.2 s. Empty reserve → no relight ("find an island").
Reserve baseMaxCups 20 (+ thermos upgrades). Docking Refill() → cups full, warmth 1.
HUD: bar only, no number (design rule).
UE: Timeline/tick for decay; the lantern is a Point/Spot light whose intensity binds to warmth; warmth → MPC scalar for the water lantern tint.

4.9 Progression & save — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/Progression/TemporaryRunState.cs
fishCount (soft currency), currentDay, activeBoatId, owned items, per-species catch counts, discoveries.
TotalCaught = lifetime sum (HUD catch tally). AddFish/TrySpendFish/RecordCatch/RecordDiscovery/AdvanceDay/ResetProgress.
JSON save to disk (dictionaries stored as parallel lists). Autosaves on quit/pause and key events.
UE: USaveGame object + SaveGameToSlot. Singleton = a GameInstance subsystem.

4.10 Upgrades / economy — _HEARTSTREAM/Scripts/Progression/WorldUpgrades.cs + UI shop
Upgrades read by other systems: BonusCups (thermos), LanternBrightness, fish-density multiplier, etc.
Bought with fishCount via the dock shop (ShopController / DockVesselController).

All translucent, theme in UITheme.cs (panel #0A0E14@.5, accent warm gold #D9B36C).

CompassBar — scrolling cardinal strip (N/E/S/W + ticks) past a fixed pointer, north-up. Top-center.

4.12 Atmosphere — mood + audio
AtmosphereMoodController — 6 mood presets; lerps fog, directional light, water tint, _HsDaylight.
AtmosphereAudioController — 6 layered sources (music, ocean, wind, rain, underwater, lantern), 5 snapshots blended by weather/time. All cross-faded, never cut.
UE: Post Process Volume + a lighting/“sky” blend driven by a mood curve; MetaSounds or a layered audio component with parameter-driven mixing.

4.13 Weather & biomes
WeatherEffectsController — rain/snow particles; storm ≠ rain (denser, faster, wind-skewed, darker). Known issue to fix in port: particles should spawn only in a radius around the boat, not as a full-sea field.
Biomes: paradise / open / storm / arctic — each its own weather, fish set, light, sea state.
BiomeState, IceFloeField (drifting ice in arctic; collision at speed ends the voyage).
Chunk/region types: EmptyOcean, SafeShallows, MidDepth, DeepWater, IslandCluster (distance-based depth zones).
RegionController — named locations (e.g. "Sunlit Shallows / Grayhaven"), minimap labels, weather warnings.
UE: Niagara for weather (emitter follows the pawn). Biome = data asset selected by region; drives weather, fish table, lighting preset, wave amplitude.

4.14 Encounters & monster
OceanEncounterDirector — rhythm spawner: minor life every 20–45 s, mystery every 180–360 s.
Deep-water monster (MonsterController): in deep zones, continuing to move triggers it to drag you under. Stay still / cut engine to survive. Death = respawn at dock, catch reset to 0.
FishSchoolController — boid-like ambient fish, 3 presets (Circular, Traveling, Sparse).
UE: Director = a timer-driven subsystem. Monster = behavior triggered by sustained speed in a deep biome; ties back to the same "be still" verb as fishing.
